How to use TrackED

The frame

Make an L with the thumb and index of each hand. Everything inside the frame changes. Pinch it shut and reopen quickly to switch effect.

One-hand signs

👍 hold for a photo · ✌️ next effect · 🖐 freeze the frame.

Music mode

Switch Effects → Music at the top of the panel (or M). 8 instruments: Theremin, Keys, Harp, Drums, Chords, Arp, Beats and DJ. Never played music? Open Learn: first steps, songs that wait for you, rhythm games and music basics. Band adds drums and bass in your key. Loop with L.

Masks & 3D

In the Mask and 3D tabs. By default they appear when the frame passes over a face.

Keyboard

← → effects · P photo · R record · F freeze · D tracking · ? help

Tracking tips

Good light, hands fully in view, about an arm's length from the camera. A plain background helps.

Make it yours

Step-by-step guides for your own image, mask, face swap, 3D model and colour.

Capture

The photo and video buttons use a 3 s timer by default (both hands are busy!). Change it in More.
